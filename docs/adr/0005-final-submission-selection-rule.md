# Final submission is chosen by local score, with the leaderboard as a veto

The challenge requires us to commit to exactly one final submission before seeing private scores, and that submission is scored in both prize rounds. We fix the rule now rather than deciding on gut feel at the deadline. We pick the submission with the best local score unless its leaderboard score is clearly worse than the runner-up's. The local score covers far more of the region than the public test chunk, so it is the more trustworthy signal (see ADR 0001).
